### 📂 Featured Projects

#### 🏦 [RBI Monetary Policy & Inflation Analysis](https://github.com/virajrank48-max/macro-pro)
> **Python · OLS Regression · Time Series · Statsmodels**

Analysed the lagged impact of RBI's repo rate on inflation using official RBI DBIE data. Applied OLS regression with lagged variables to model monetary policy transmission delays — findings align with RBI's documented transmission mechanism.

- 📊 EDA on monthly macroeconomic time-series data
- 📉 OLS regression with lagged repo rate variables
- 🔍 Results show statistically significant policy-inflation lag

---

#### 🌍 [Democratic Destabilization & Militarization Analysis](https://github.com/virajrank48-max/country-specific)
> **STATA · Econometrics · World Bank Data · Cross-country Panel**

Built a cross-country dataset covering **68 countries** using World Bank indicators to study the relationship between militarization and democratic stability. Performed data cleaning, variable construction, and robustness checks.

- 🌐 68-country cross-sectional dataset from World Bank
- 📐 Econometric modelling of political-military dynamics
- ✅ Robustness checks and variable construction in STATA

---

#### 📈 [Economic Growth Modelling: Solow–Swan & RCK Model (South Korea)](https://github.com/virajrank48-max/projects)
> **Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Econometrics**

Collected South Korea macroeconomic time-series data (GDP, capital formation, population growth), estimated a Cobb–Douglas production function and Barro regression, then implemented the Solow–Swan and Ramsey–Cass–Koopmans models to simulate capital dynamics and convergence.

- 🇰🇷 Real macroeconomic data for South Korea
- 📊 Cobb–Douglas production function estimation
- 📉 Capital gap and convergence visualisation
